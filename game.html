How to Play Sync Room

A fast social word deduction game for 3+ players.

1
Hold to Peek Your Word Hold the vault card (or Spacebar) to view your word safely. Release immediately to hide from nearby eyes.
2
Drop Subtle Clues Take turns typing a short clue. In Similar Word mode, nobody knows who has the slightly different word!
3
Vote & Score Voting for the real Imposter earns +2 pts (even on ties/misses). If the Imposter escapes, they earn +3 pts!
