How to Play Sync Room

A fast social word deduction game for 3+ players.

1
Peek Your Secret Word Hold the vault card to view your word privately. In Similar Word mode, one player secretly gets a slightly different word — and nobody knows who!
2
Give Turn-Based Clues Take turns typing a subtle 1-word or short clue. Be specific enough to prove you belong, but vague enough not to help the Imposter!
3
Vote & Score Points Correctly voting for the Imposter earns +2 pts (even if the group misses!). If the Imposter escapes elimination, they earn +3 pts.
